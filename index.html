Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28.10.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameStudioH2O ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the HeroMe! mobile application (the "App"). For questions, contact GameStudioH2O@gmail.com.

1. Gameplay and local data

HeroMe! does not require an account. Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this locally stored gameplay information through the App's core functionality. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it may remove this data.

2. Advertising and data processed by third parties

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. When advertising services are active, Google and its partners may automatically collect or process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic information. Depending on configuration and applicable law, this information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where permitted.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Internet access

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. An internet connection may be used to request and display ads and to support related advertising services.

4. Data sharing

Advertising-related information may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners through AdMob as described above. We do not independently sell personal information collected through the App's core gameplay. The way advertising data is handled depends on the advertising configuration, user choices, and applicable laws.

5. Privacy choices and retention

You can manage some advertising preferences and reset or delete your device's advertising identifier through supported device settings. Where required, the App will provide applicable advertising consent or privacy choices. Google and its partners control retention of data they process under their respective policies. You may contact us using the email below for privacy questions or requests.

6. Intended audience

HeroMe! is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older. It is not designed specifically for children under 13.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it transmits in transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or the App change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date.
